When Conflicts Redraw the Skies

A short-term research and visualization project exploring how geopolitical conflicts alter global aviation routes.

Blog Visualizations

The Problem

Airspace closures caused by wars and political instability force airlines to reroute, increasing fuel burn, flight time, emissions, and economic cost. These impacts are fragmented across datasets and rarely visualized together.

Current Capabilities

Route Deviation Analysis

Compare peace-time vs conflict-time flight paths across regions and timelines.

Density & Heat Maps

Visualize congestion shifts and airspace pressure due to restricted zones.

Conflict Correlation

Overlay geopolitical events directly on aviation and logistics data.

Future Scope

Emissions Impact

Estimate additional CO₂ emissions caused by prolonged rerouting.
